Baby Face Wash

Baby Face Wash (Sulfate-Free)

Cleanse your baby’s delicate facial skin gently with our Sulfate-Free Baby Face Wash, specially formulated to remove impurities without causing dryness or irritation. Enriched with soothing and nourishing ingredients, it keeps the skin soft, hydrated, and healthy.

    Key Benefits:
  • Gently cleanses without harsh sulfates
  • Maintains skin hydration and softness
  • Soothes and protects sensitive skin
  • Safe for daily use on delicate baby skin
    Active Ingredients:
  • Coco-Glucoside - Mild, plant-based cleanser that gently removes dirt and impurities.
  • Decyl Glucoside - Gentle, non-irritating surfactant ideal for sensitive skin.
  • Aloe Vera Extract - Soothes and hydrates delicate skin.
  • Glycerin - Locks in moisture to keep skin soft and smooth.
  • Chamomile Extract - Calms irritation and reduces redness.
  • Panthenol - Supports skin barrier health and hydration.
How to Use:

1. Apply a small amount to wet baby’s face.

2. Gently massage in circular motions to cleanse.

3. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

4. Use daily for soft, clean, and healthy skin.

Who Can Use It:

Safe for all babies and toddlers, including those with sensitive skin. Ideal for gentle daily facial cleansing without irritation.
