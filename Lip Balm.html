Lip Balm SPF 15

Our Lip Balm with SPF 15 is specially formulated to nourish, hydrate, and protect your lips from everyday damage. Enriched with natural butters and oils, it provides deep moisturization while the broad-spectrum SPF 15 shields delicate lips from harmful UVA & UVB rays. Lightweight and non-greasy, it glides on smoothly and keeps lips soft, supple, and protected all day long.

    Key Benefits
  • Sun Protection - Shields lips from harmful sun exposure with SPF 15.
  • Deep Moisturization – Infused with shea butter, cocoa butter, and natural oils to prevent dryness and chapping.
  • Healing & Repair – Helps soothe cracked lips and restore natural softness.
  • Non-Sticky Formula – Lightweight, smooth, and easy to apply anytime.
  • Everyday Use - Suitable for all skin types and safe for daily application.
    Active Ingredients (Example blend, can adjust to your actual formula)
  • Shea Butter – Provides deep hydration and nourishment.
  • Cocoa Butter – Softens and smooths lips.
  • Beeswax / Candelilla Wax (vegan option) – Locks in moisture and protects.
  • Vitamin E – Powerful antioxidant for healing and repair.
  • SPF Filters (Zinc Oxide / Titanium Dioxide or approved sunscreen agents) / Lactic AciUread – Protects from sunburn and UV damage.
  • Natural Oils (Coconut Oil, Almond Oil, Jojoba Oil, Castor Oil) Adds shine, smoothness, and moisture balance.
How to Use

Apply generously on lips whenever needed. Reapply every 2–3 hours, especially after eating, drinking, or sun exposure.
