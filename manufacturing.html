Our Plant

KUKU Cosmetics’ manufacturing plant is a modern, state-of-the-art facility located in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Equipped with advanced machinery, skilled professionals, and a strong R&D department, we ensure the production of high-quality cosmetics that meet international standards.

Our facility also features well-planned infrastructure and an eco-friendly work environment, enabling us to excel in the private labelling sector and deliver innovative, reliable products to our clients.

Highlights of our manufacturing plant

  • FDA approved R.C.C. production unit
  • Adherence to the GMP/ ISO/ USFDA standards of manufacturing
  • All manufacturing and filling areas maintained under class 1,00,000 (HEPA Filters) with vitrified tiles and all walls of production area are covered with glaze tiles, which ensures cleanliness and hygiene
  • Separate man and material entry air locks for each manufacturing area as well as filling rooms
  • FDA Approved Cosmetics Unit.
  • All Vessels, Machines and Contract Parts are Made with SS-316 To Prevent Material Reaction & To Maintain Batch Hygiene.
  • Segregation of every critical processing activity in each zone, to avoid cross-contamination
  • Each zone has separate dehumidification unit and dust extraction systems
  • Hi-tech laboratory setup for product innovation and testing
  • CCTV camera setup throughout the factory for complete safety and security
  • Respective zones, areas and even uniforms marked with specific colours to ensure total segregation
  • Duo Pass Reverse Osmosis (RO) water system/ de-mineralization plant
  • Zero-discharge Effluent Treatment Plant
  • Fully computerized individual/ separate office administrative building with latest communication facilities along with server room

Accreditation & Compliance

At KUKU Cosmetics, we believe that true quality goes beyond great formulations — it is about trust, safety, and compliance. That’s why our manufacturing facility is built on globally recognized standards and accredited systems.

    Our Accreditations & Standards
  • GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices): Ensures hygienic, safe, and consistent manufacturing processes.
  • ISO Certification: Implementation of international quality management systems for efficiency and reliability.
  • Indian FDA Compliance: Approved facility operating under the guidelines of the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940.
  • Safety & Hygiene Standards: Strict adherence to health, safety, and environmental regulations.
    • Quality & Safety Commitment
  • Every raw material and finished product undergoes QC & microbiological testing before release.
  • Dedicated QA team ensures compliance with both domestic and export market requirements.
  • Documentation practices aligned with ISO 22716 (Cosmetics GMP guidelines)

Research & Innovation

At KUKU Cosmetics, innovation drives everything we do. Our Research & Development (R&D) team works tirelessly to develop high-performance, safe, and trend-driven cosmetic products that meet the evolving needs of brands and consumers alike.

    Our R&D Capabilities
  • Custom Formulation Development: Tailored solutions for skincare, hair care, body care, oral care, and intimate care.
  • Pilot-Scale Testing: Small batch trials to validate stability, texture, fragrance, and performance before full-scale production.
  • Advanced Laboratory Equipment: High-shear mixers, homogenizers, stability chambers, and analytical instruments for precise formulation development.
  • Ingredient Innovation: Use of safe, natural, and scientifically proven ingredients to create effective cosmetic solutions.
  • Trend Analysis: Monitoring global cosmetic trends to ensure products are market-ready and consumer-relevant.
    Our Innovation Process
  • 1. Idea & Concept Generation
  • 2. Formulation Development
  • 3. Pilot Batch Production
  • 4. Scale-Up & Manufacturing Support
