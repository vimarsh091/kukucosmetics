Quality Assurance

Quality Department

At KUKU Cosmetic, quality is at the heart of everything we do. Our dedicated Quality Department ensures that every product we manufacture meets the highest standards of safety, efficacy, and compliance. The department is structured into three key divisions:

Research & Development (R&D)

Our R&D team is the backbone of innovation.

  • Expertise in customized formulations for all cosmetic products.

  • Development of innovative, trend-driven cosmetic products

  • Conducts compatibility & stability studies under different conditions

  • Works with dermatologically safe and sustainable ingredients

  • Keeps updated with latest cosmetic science & global market trends

  • Provides prototype samples for client evaluation before scaling up

    Quality Control (QC)

    The QC department ensures that raw materials, packaging components, and finished products are rigorously tested before release.

  • Strict sampling and testing of raw materials before production

  • In-process monitoring at every manufacturing stage

  • Verification of packaging materials for durability & compliance

  • Maintains batch-to-batch consistency with precision testing

  • Immediate corrective action in case of any deviation

    Quality Assurance (QA)

    Our QA team makes sure every product complies with global regulatory and quality standards.

  • GMP, GLP & GDP compliance in all operations

  • Strict documentation and record-keeping practices

  • Batch-wise review, approval, and certification

  • Internal audits and continuous process improvement

  • Ensuring regulatory support for domestic and international markets

With this 3-tier quality approach (R&D + QC + QA), we deliver safe, effective, and high-performing cosmetic products trusted by brands.
