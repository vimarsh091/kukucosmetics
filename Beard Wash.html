Beard Wash

Enriched with Aloe Vera Extract (10%), Vitamin E (0.1%), and Tea Tree Oil (1%), this gentle yet effective beard wash is designed to deeply cleanse while maintaining natural moisture. It leaves your beard feeling clean, healthy, soft, and fluffy with a refreshing scent.

  • Deeply cleanses without stripping natural oils
  • Softens and smooths beard hair for a fluffy, well-groomed look
  • Aloe Vera soothes and hydrates
  • Vitamin E nourishes and protects
  • Tea Tree Oil keeps your beard fresh and healthy
