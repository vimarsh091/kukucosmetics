Vaginal Tightening & Firming Cream

Our Vaginal Tightening Cream is carefully formulated with herbal extracts, peptides, and hydrating actives to help restore elasticity, improve firmness, and support intimate wellness. Gentle yet effective, it provides natural tightening, nourishment, and long-lasting freshness.

    Key Active Ingredients & Benefits
  • Aloe Vera Extract – Deeply hydrates, soothes, and calms delicate skin.
  • Manjakani Extract - Known for its traditional use in feminine care, it helps tighten, tone, and rejuvenate skin
  • Witch Hazel Extract – Acts as a natural astringent, improving firmness and supporting elasticity.
  • Vitamin E – Protects skin from oxidative damage while keeping it soft and nourished.
  • Collagen Peptides – Enhances skin structure, restores elasticity, and improves suppleness.
  • Panthenol - Moisturizes, repairs, and strengthens the skin barrier for lasting comfort.
