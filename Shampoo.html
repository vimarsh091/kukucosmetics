Shampoo

Our advanced Hair Shampoo range is formulated to gently cleanse, nourish, and protect the scalp and hair, leaving it soft, strong, and revitalized. Infused with premium active ingredients, each formula is designed to target specific hair concerns while maintaining scalp health.

    Key Benefits
  • Gently removes dirt, oil, and buildup without stripping natural moisture
  • Strengthens hair from root to tip, reducing breakage and hair fall
  • Soothes and balances the scalp, helping control dandruff and irritation
  • Restores shine, smoothness, and manageability for healthier-looking hair
  • Provides UV and pollution protection to prevent environmental damage
    Active Ingredients (customizable as per client needs)
  • Keratin & Biotin – to strengthen and repair damaged strands
  • Aloe Vera & Panthenol – for hydration and scalp soothing
  • Zinc Pyrithione / Ketoconazole – for dandruff and scalp care
  • Natural Oils (Argan, Coconut, Jojoba) – to deeply nourish and add shine
  • Niacinamide & Peptides – to support hair density and resilience
How to Use

1. Apply a small amount of shampoo to wet hair and scalp.

2. Massage gently in circular motions to create a rich lather.

3. Rinse thoroughly with water.

4. For best results, follow with a conditioner or hair serum from our range.

With consistent use, hair feels clean, healthy, and full of life.
