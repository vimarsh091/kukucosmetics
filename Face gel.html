Face Gel

Our Face Gel is a lightweight, fast-absorbing formula designed to deliver intense hydration and targeted skin benefits without leaving any sticky residue. Enriched with Hyaluronic Acid, Aloe Vera, Niacinamide, Vitamin C, Green Tea Extract, and Panthenol,it provides an instant cooling effect while nourishing and revitalizing the skin.

    Key Benefits
  • Deep hydrationwith lightweight, non-greasy texture
  • Soothes irritation and calms sensitive or sun-exposed skin
  • Brightens and evens skin tonefor a radiant glow
  • Controls excess oilwhile keeping skin moisturized
  • Reduces fine lines & fatigue signswith daily use
  • Suitable for all skin typesincluding oily and acne-prone skin
How to Use

1. Cleanse and tone your face.

2. Take a small amount of gel and apply evenly over the face and neck.

3. Gently massage until fully absorbed.

4. Use morning and evening, or as needed for hydration and soothing care.

With consistent use, your skin feels fresh, hydrated, and naturally radiant all day long.
