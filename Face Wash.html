Face Wash

A revolutionary cleanser designed for on-the-go freshness without the need for water. Our Waterless Face Wash gently removes dirt, excess oil, and impurities while leaving the skin soft, clean, and refreshed.

Formulated with mild surfactants like Coco-Glucoside, soothing botanicals, and hydrating agents, it offers a quick and convenient cleansing solution—perfect for travel, hectic lifestyles, and outdoor activities.

    Benefits
  • Cleanses without water –no rinsing required
  • Maintainsskin hydration and softness
  • Gentle and safe for daily use
  • Perfect for Radiance boost:travel, work, or outdoor activities
How to Use

Apply a small amount to face, massage gently in circular motions, and wipe away with a soft tissue or cotton pad — no rinsing needed.
